ABOUT THE COURSE

What is Fundamentals of Computing?

Fundamentals of Computing introduces students to the basic ideas behind computers and information technology. Students explore how computers represent and process data, how hardware and software work together, and how computer networks allow devices to communicate.

These concepts are useful in everyday life and in many careers because they help people understand, use, and solve problems with technology.
